Each semester has two internal tests and one end-semester examination. Internal tests carry 40 marks and the end-semester examination carries 60 marks.

A student needs at least 75 percent attendance in a subject to be allowed to write the end-semester examination for that subject.

The minimum passing mark is 40 out of 100 in every subject. Students who fail a subject can write the supplementary examination held in July.

Students must reach the examination hall 15 minutes before the exam starts and carry their hall ticket and college ID card. Calculators are allowed only if the question paper says so.

Results are published on the college website about 30 days after the last examination. Students can apply for revaluation within 7 days of the results by paying 500 rupees per subject.
