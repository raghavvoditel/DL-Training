The tuition fee for the B.Tech programme is 1,20,000 rupees per year. The hostel fee is 60,000 rupees per year including mess charges.

Fees must be paid before 31 July for the odd semester and before 31 December for the even semester. A late fee of 100 rupees per day is charged after the deadline.

Fees can be paid online through the student portal using UPI, net banking or a debit card. A receipt is emailed to the student after payment.

Students with family income below 2.5 lakh rupees a year can apply for a fee concession scholarship at the accounts office before 15 August.
