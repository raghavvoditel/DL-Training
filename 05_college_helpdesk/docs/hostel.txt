The college has separate hostels for boys and girls with a total of 600 rooms. Rooms are shared by two students and include a bed, a study table and a cupboard.

Hostel gates close at 10:00 pm every night. Students returning later must inform the warden in advance.

The mess serves breakfast from 7:00 am to 9:00 am, lunch from 12:30 pm to 2:00 pm and dinner from 7:30 pm to 9:30 pm. Both vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals are available.

Visitors are allowed in the hostel visiting room between 4:00 pm and 7:00 pm. Visitors are not allowed inside student rooms.
