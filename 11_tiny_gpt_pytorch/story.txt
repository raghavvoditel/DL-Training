once upon a time there was a little robot who lived in a quiet village.
every morning the little robot watered the flowers and fed the birds.
the people of the village loved the robot because it was kind and helpful.
one day a big storm came and the river began to rise.
the little robot carried the children to the top of the hill.
it worked all night to build a wall of stones near the river.
when the sun came out the village was safe and the people cheered.
the children gave the robot a crown made of flowers.
from that day the robot was called the guardian of the village.
the robot smiled and said that helping others is the best kind of magic.
the birds sang and the flowers danced in the morning light.
and the little robot lived happily in the quiet village forever.
